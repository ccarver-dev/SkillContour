# SkillContour

https://skillcontourv01.netlify.app/manager

**See where skills are strong, where gaps are forming, and what support should come next.**

SkillContour is a workforce readiness, onboarding, training, and coaching platform created by **New Methods Lab**.

---

## What SkillContour Does

Most training systems show whether an assignment was completed. SkillContour goes further by helping leaders understand whether someone can apply the skill, where support is needed, and whether coaching made a difference.

SkillContour brings training, assessment results, confidence, coaching, evidence, and reporting into one connected experience.

It helps leaders answer four practical questions:

1. What is happening?
2. Why is it happening?
3. What support should come next?
4. Did the coaching work?

---

## Manager Control Center

The Manager Control Center gives leaders a clear view of team and individual development.

Managers can:

- Review team and individual readiness
- Identify skill gaps and emerging patterns
- Open learner profiles for deeper context
- Review training progress and assessment results
- Compare confidence with accuracy
- Document coaching and follow-up actions
- Add evidence that supports observations
- Review reports and trends

---

## People

The People area gives managers a focused view of each learner.

Managers can review:

- Assigned and completed training
- Assessment performance
- Confidence patterns
- Coaching history
- Supporting evidence
- Areas that may need follow-up

---

## Training

The Training area allows managers to assign learning and monitor progress.

Managers can:

- View available training
- Assign training to learners
- Track progress
- Review completion and assessment results
- Connect training activity to individual development

---

## Learner Experience

The learner experience gives each person a clear path through assigned training.

Learners can:

- View assigned training
- Continue training already in progress
- Complete lessons and knowledge checks
- Rate confidence while answering
- Use available learning resources
- See what needs attention next

Confidence and accuracy are captured together, giving managers more context than completion alone.

---

## Training Builder

The Training Builder allows training creators to build structured learning experiences.

Creators can:

- Build lessons and assessments
- Add questions and answer choices
- Include confidence ratings
- Organize content into clear sections
- Connect training activity to reporting

---

## Coaching

Coaching is built into the workflow instead of being tracked separately.

Managers can document:

- The skill or behavior being addressed
- Why support is needed
- The coaching action taken
- Follow-up expectations
- Whether improvement occurred

This helps connect manager action to learner outcomes.

---

## Evidence

The Evidence area gives managers a place to document the information behind a decision.

Evidence may include:

- Manager observations
- Work examples
- Assessment results
- Resource use
- Coaching notes
- Operational outcomes

This makes decisions easier to understand, support, and revisit.

---

## Data

The Data area brings together the signals captured throughout the platform.

These signals can include:

- Training activity
- Assessment accuracy
- Confidence ratings
- Coaching activity
- Evidence
- Individual and team patterns

---

## Reports

Reports help leaders move from activity to action.

They can be used to review:

- Training progress
- Accuracy and confidence patterns
- Individual and team skill gaps
- Coaching activity
- Evidence collected
- Changes over time

---

## Why SkillContour

SkillContour is built around a simple idea:

> Training should not stop at completion. Leaders need to see what people understand, where support is needed, and whether the support worked.

The platform is designed for organizations that want a more systematic, practical, and evidence-based approach to onboarding, training, coaching, and workforce development.
