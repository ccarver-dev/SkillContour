# SkillContour

**See where skills are strong, where gaps are forming, and what to do next.**

SkillContour is a workforce readiness, onboarding, training, and coaching platform created by **New Methods Lab**.

[Explore the SkillContour demo](https://skillcontourv4.netlify.app/login)

## Why SkillContour

Most training systems can tell a leader who completed an assignment. Completion alone does not show whether someone can apply the skill, where support is needed, or whether coaching made a difference.

SkillContour brings learning activity, assessment results, confidence, resource use, manager observations, coaching, and operational evidence into one readiness model. It is designed to complement learning and quality systems, not replace them.

The platform helps answer four practical questions:

1. What is happening?
2. Why is it happening?
3. What support should come next?
4. Did the coaching work?

## How It Works

SkillContour combines evidence from sources such as:

- Rise or another LMS
- Native knowledge checks and practical scenarios
- Question level confidence ratings
- First and final attempt results
- Approved resource use
- Manager observations and coaching
- CallMiner or other quality data
- Future operational and performance sources

## Manager Control Center

Managers can move from a team level signal to the evidence behind it, then take action without losing context.

| Area | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | See current readiness, emerging gaps, and priorities |
| People | Review individual and training group readiness |
| Training | Assign learning and monitor progress |
| Coaching | Record actions, notes, follow ups, and outcomes |
| Evidence | Inspect the signals behind a readiness judgment |
| Training Builder | Create lessons, checks, scenarios, and confidence prompts |
| Data | Review the information feeding the readiness model |
| Reports | Analyze patterns and export useful results |

Manager capabilities include:

- Team and individual readiness views
- Accuracy compared with confidence
- First attempt compared with final attempt improvement
- Completion, recency, resource use, and skill evidence
- Coaching notes, action steps, and follow up tracking
- Training assignments and content creation
- Reports and filtered exports
- Role based manager and learner experiences

## Learner Workspace

The learner experience gives each person a clear path forward without exposing manager only information.

Learners can:

- View assigned training paths, lessons, tasks, and due dates
- Complete knowledge checks and practical scenarios
- Rate confidence at the point of learning
- Use approved resources while working through training
- Review progress, feedback, and material that needs attention
- Leave and resume training without losing their place

## Readiness Terrain

Readiness Terrain turns skill evidence into a true contour map of a training group.

- **Peaks** represent demonstrated strengths
- **Valleys** represent demonstrated support needs
- **Gray or hatched areas** represent insufficient evidence, not poor performance

The terrain uses one learner by skill grid, with learners organized by row and skills by column. Shared 2D and 3D views reveal patterns across a training group, while searchable learner and skill paths support closer review.

Clickable points explain the evidence behind the terrain, including accuracy, confidence, attempts, improvement, completion, recency, and resource use. An accessible table view provides the same underlying information without requiring the visual map.

Readiness Terrain is built for coaching and decision support. It is not an employee leaderboard and is not intended to rank people against one another.

## Designed For

SkillContour is designed for organizations that need earlier and clearer signs of readiness, including:

- Small businesses building a repeatable onboarding process
- Growing companies without a complete training data structure
- Call centers and customer support teams
- Sales and service organizations
- Managers who need useful indicators before formal performance data arrives

## Product Principles

- Completion is not the same as readiness
- Confidence and accuracy are more useful when viewed together
- Evidence should lead to a clear coaching action
- Human judgment should never be replaced by an unexplained score
- Missing evidence should not be treated as weak performance
- Readiness views should support people, not rank them
- Visual insights should always have an accessible alternative
- Manager, learner, and organization data must remain appropriately separated

## Project Status

SkillContour is an actively developed, functional product demonstration. The public demo uses representative data to show the manager and learner workflows.

External platforms named in this README describe supported or planned evidence sources. They do not imply that every external integration is connected in the public demo. A production implementation would require organization specific integrations, role and tenant configuration, security review, and deployment.
