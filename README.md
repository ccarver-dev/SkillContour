Two connected experiences
SkillContour is designed as two connected applications that share one source of readiness evidence.
Learner Workspace
The learner experience provides:
A clear training path with defined next steps
Assigned courses, lessons, tasks, and due dates
Approved resources in one organized location
Knowledge checks and practical scenarios
Interactive confidence ratings with scored questions
Progress across the skills required for the role
Useful feedback and recommended review material
The ability to leave and resume training
Learners see their own training, progress, resources, and shared feedback. They do not have access to private manager notes, team analytics, or manager controls.
Manager Control Center
The manager experience provides:
Team and individual readiness views
Training assignments and progress tracking
Skill level evidence
Confidence compared with accuracy
First attempt and final attempt improvement
Coaching notes, follow ups, and recommended actions
Training creation and assignment tools
Reports and filtered exports
Access to the Readiness Terrain
Managers can examine the evidence behind a readiness result and use it to make a better coaching decision. SkillContour is not designed to rank employees from best to worst.
Signature feature: Readiness Terrain
The Readiness Terrain turns workforce readiness into an interactive skill landscape.
Peaks represent demonstrated strengths
Valleys represent areas that need support
Gray areas identify insufficient evidence
Clickable skill points explain the data behind each result
Team and individual views help managers find shared and specific needs
Learner comparison shows differences in coaching needs without creating a leaderboard
Baseline and current views reveal improvement over time
The terrain is not decorative. Each point is intended to connect to real evidence such as accuracy, confidence, attempts, improvement, completion, recency, and resource use.
Confidence is part of the evidence
SkillContour captures a confidence rating with each scored answer before the learner sees whether the answer is correct.
Answer pattern
What it may reveal
Correct with high confidence
Likely established understanding
Correct with low confidence
Knowledge that may need reinforcement
Incorrect with low confidence
A recognized learning gap
Incorrect with high confidence
A possible misconception and higher coaching priority
These patterns are signals, not final judgments. Managers should always be able to review the underlying answers, activity, and sample size before acting.
Readiness evidence
SkillContour is designed to bring together evidence such as:
Demonstrated answer accuracy
Scenario and practical performance
First attempt and final attempt improvement
Completion of required work
Confidence calibration
Repeated performance and retention when available
Recent activity
Appropriate use of approved resources
Manager reviewed practical work
Confidence alone never creates a readiness peak. When there is not enough evidence, SkillContour should say so instead of inventing certainty.
Core capabilities
Training management: Organize courses, modules, lessons, questions, videos, documents, and approved resources.
Progress tracking: Show completed work, active assignments, due dates, and next steps.
Confidence capture: Record how sure a learner is before answer feedback is revealed.
Attempt history: Preserve first and final answers so improvement is not erased.
Skills visibility: Connect training activity to the abilities required for the role.
Readiness insights: Identify developing gaps in knowledge, confidence, judgment, and execution.
Coaching support: Turn evidence into notes, assigned resources, follow up training, and next actions.
Team reporting: Reveal patterns across learners, roles, departments, and cohorts.
Accessible alternatives: Provide a clear two dimensional and table view of terrain data.
Designed for real workplace training
SkillContour can support:
Small businesses building their first repeatable onboarding process
Growing companies whose training has become scattered
Call centers and customer support teams
Sales and service organizations
Managers who need useful early indicators before formal performance data is available
Teams that need training built around their own work instead of a one size fits all template
Product principles
Clear expectations and next steps help people succeed.
Completion is not the same as readiness.
Confidence and accuracy should be examined together.
Training evidence should lead to a useful coaching action.
Readiness data should support human judgment, not replace it.
Employees should never be reduced to one unexplained score.
Technology should make training clearer, not create another complicated system to maintain.
Appropriate resource use is a real workplace skill, not an automatic weakness.
Current status
SkillContour is an interactive product prototype in active development.
The current work demonstrates the learner and manager experiences, training organization, confidence capture, readiness analysis, coaching workflow, and Readiness Terrain concept. It is not yet represented as a production system.
SkillContour does not currently claim direct integration with CRM, email, telephony, reservation, HR, or other enterprise platforms. Future implementations can be adapted around a business's existing tools, roles, terminology, training content, and workflow.
