SkillContour

See where skills are strong, where gaps are forming, and what to do next.

SkillContour is a workforce readiness, onboarding, training, and coaching platform created by New Methods Lab.

"Explore the SkillContour demo" (https://skillcontourv4.netlify.app/login)

Why SkillContour

Most training systems can tell a leader who completed an assignment. Completion alone does not show whether someone can apply the skill, where support is needed, or whether coaching made a difference.

SkillContour brings learning activity, assessment results, confidence, resource use, manager observations, coaching, and operational evidence into one readiness model.

The platform helps answer four practical questions:

1. What is happening?
2. Why is it happening?
3. What support should come next?
4. Did the coaching work?

Manager Control Center

Managers can move from a team signal to the evidence behind it and take action without losing context.

SkillContour includes:

- Team and individual readiness views
- Training assignments and progress tracking
- Accuracy compared with confidence
- First attempt compared with final attempt improvement
- Coaching notes, action steps, and follow-up tracking
- Evidence review
- Training creation tools
- Reports and filtered exports

Learner Workspace

The learner experience gives each person a clear path forward without exposing manager-only information.

Learners can:

- View assigned training, tasks, and due dates
- Complete knowledge checks and practical scenarios
- Rate confidence before receiving answer feedback
- Use approved resources during training
- Review progress and feedback
  

Designed For

SkillContour is designed for organizations that need earlier and clearer signs of readiness, including:

- Small businesses building a repeatable onboarding process
- Growing companies with scattered training systems
- Call centers and customer-support teams
- Sales and service organizations
- Managers who need useful indicators before formal performance data becomes available

Product Principles

- Completion is not the same as readiness
- Confidence and accuracy are more useful when viewed together
- Evidence should lead to a clear coaching action
- Human judgment should never be replaced by an unexplained score
- Missing evidence should not be treated as weak performance
- Readiness views should support people, not rank them
- Manager and learner information must remain appropriately separated

Project Status

SkillContour is an actively developed, functional product demonstration. The public demo uses representative data to demonstrate the manager and learner workflows.

A production implementation would require organization-specific integrations, role configuration, security review, and deployment.

Creator

SkillContour was created by Christopher Carver through New Methods Lab as a practical response to fragmented onboarding, unclear training signals, and coaching that is difficult to measure.

The goal is simple: make readiness easier to see, easier to explain, and easier to act on.
