# SkillContour

### Workforce readiness made visible

> See where skills are strong, where gaps are forming, and what support should come next.

SkillContour is an onboarding, training, coaching, and workforce readiness platform created by **New Methods Lab**.

**[Explore the SkillContour demo →](https://skillcontourv4.netlify.app/login)**

---

## From Training Activity to Practical Action

Most training systems show whether an assignment was completed. Completion alone does not show whether someone can apply the skill, how confident they are, where support is needed, or whether coaching worked.

SkillContour connects learner assignments, confidence, accuracy, coaching, evidence, training creation, and reporting in one workspace.

It helps leaders answer four practical questions:

1. **What is happening?**
2. **Why is it happening?**
3. **What support should come next?**
4. **Did the coaching work?**

---

## Manager Workspace

The manager experience connects team level signals with the learner details and evidence behind them.

| Area | What It Provides |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Overview** | Current readiness signals, priorities, and recent activity |
| **People** | Individual progress, confidence, accuracy, and support needs |
| **Training** | Learning assignments and progress monitoring |
| **Coaching** | Coaching activity, next steps, and follow up |
| **Evidence** | Information supporting readiness and coaching decisions |
| **Training Builder** | Tools for creating content, questions, and confidence prompts |
| **Data** | Visibility into the information feeding the platform |
| **Reports** | Pattern analysis and useful exports |

---

## Learner Workspace

The learner experience provides a focused and easy to follow training path.

Learners can:

- View assigned training and continue where they left off
- Complete knowledge checks and practical questions
- Rate their confidence while answering questions
- Review progress and feedback
- See what training needs attention next

Manager only notes, team analytics, and administrative controls remain separate from the learner experience.

---

## Connected Readiness Signals

SkillContour brings together signals that are more useful when viewed as a complete picture.

| Signal | What It Helps Reveal |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Completion** | Whether required learning was finished |
| **Accuracy** | Whether the learner demonstrated correct understanding |
| **Confidence** | How certain the learner felt while answering |
| **Attempts** | Whether understanding improved through the learning process |
| **Coaching** | What support was provided and what should happen next |
| **Evidence** | The information behind a readiness decision |

These signals support human judgment. They do not replace it.

## Designed For

SkillContour is designed for organizations that need a clearer and more repeatable approach to onboarding, training, and coaching, including:

- Small businesses building a structured onboarding process
- Growing companies with training spread across multiple tools
- Call centers and customer support teams
- Sales and service organizations
- Managers who need useful readiness indicators before formal performance data becomes available

---

## Product Principles

- **Readiness is more than completion**
- **Confidence and accuracy are stronger when viewed together**
- **Evidence should lead to a clear coaching action**
- **Missing evidence should not be treated as poor performance**
- **Human judgment should not be replaced by an unexplained score**
- **Training information should be understandable and actionable**
- **Manager and learner information should remain appropriately separated**

---

## Project Status

SkillContour is an actively developed, functional product demonstration. The public demo uses representative data to show the manager and learner experiences.

A production implementation would require organization specific configuration, integrations, security review, and deployment.
